## 👋 About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Università della Calabria**, working toward becoming an **AI & ML Engineer**.
I like designing systems that genuinely simplify processes and give people their time back.

Alongside my studies I run **[Mavo Consulting](https://mattiavolpentesta.it)**, a freelance studio where I build custom web apps, private cloud setups and AI integrations for local small and medium businesses. I follow every phase myself: requirements with the client, design, development, deployment and maintenance.

- 🎓 BSc Computer Engineering @ Unical · Harvard **CS50x** completed
- 🎯 Next: a Master's in **AI & ML**
- 🏋️ Hybrid athlete (gym + running): discipline and continuous improvement, in training and in code
